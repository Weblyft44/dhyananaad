01 — Our story & philosophy

Rooted in Sanatan wisdom.

Dhyana Naad Yogshala is a place to explore the timeless traditions of yoga, sound and spiritual learning.

Our philosophy is inspired by the understanding that peace is not something we need to chase outside. It begins with awareness, grows through practice and unfolds through compassion.

We welcome seekers at every stage of their journey — those taking their first steps and those wishing to deepen their existing practice.

With love, awareness & devotion
Dhyana Bhandari · Guide
